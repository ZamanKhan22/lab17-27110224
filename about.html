Our story

A small place for generous cups, Peshawari flavours and stories worth carrying home.

Where every cup begins a conversation

Qissa Khwani Kahwa House grew from the old bazaar's love of gathering. Here, neighbours, travellers and friends meet over kahwa, warm kebabs and the easy rhythm of Peshawar.

We serve familiar recipes with care: cardamom in the tea, spice in the kebab and something sweet at the end. Our welcome is simple. Come as you are, stay for a while and leave with a good story.
